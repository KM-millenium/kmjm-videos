Your Phone's Map Only Works Because Einstein Was Right About Time
GPS satellites don't know where you are. They only send the time, and your phone does the math.
Up in orbit, satellite clocks gain about 38 millionths of a second a day thanks to relativity. Left unfixed, your map would drift about 10 km every day.
