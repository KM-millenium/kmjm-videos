Why Walking Through a Door Makes You Forget
Ever walk into a room and forget why you went there? Blame the doorway.
Your brain files memories by "scenes", and crossing a doorway can start a new one. The old thought gets pushed to the background.
Go back to the first room and it often comes right back.
