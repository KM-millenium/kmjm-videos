Why Do Your Fingers Prune in the Bath? It's NOT What You Think
Your skin isn't soaking up water like a sponge. Your nervous system is wrinkling your fingers on purpose.
Since the 1930s, scientists have known that fingers with cut nerves don't wrinkle at all. The leading idea: the wrinkles work like tire treads for a better wet grip (though scientists still argue about it).
